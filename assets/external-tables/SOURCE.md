# Kenney Boardgame Pack v2

Kenney Vleugels released Boardgame Pack under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. The original license is included. The official catalog is at https://kenney.nl/assets/boardgame-pack and the artist's distribution page is https://opengameart.org/content/boardgame-pack.

The source archive is https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/boardgamePack_v2.zip. Its SHA-256 is `3b6a7dd5af658d1ffa0071429d5dde10ff63121e88bd06d4904e721e60b2c398`.

The four suit sheets preserve the RGBA pixels of all 52 original PNGs under PNG/Cards, in ascending rank (A through K), seven columns per row. The die sheet preserves all six dieWhite_border PNGs under PNG/Dice, in ascending order, three columns per row. Only their arrangement into sheets was changed.
