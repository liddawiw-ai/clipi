Terms of Service

You choose what to share in a room. Respect other participants and share only content you have permission to broadcast. Room gifts spend purchased diamonds.

In PI Browser, 1,000 diamonds cost 1 PI. The external app shows its available packs and payment options before checkout.

Every 10,000 purchased diamonds received in room gifts records a 5 PI reward in the recipient’s Clipi account. Remaining diamonds carry forward.

Community rules prohibit threats, harassment, scams, impersonation, exposing private information, and child sexual abuse or exploitation. Report a violation in room settings, the participant menu or the message actions. Block the user and follow up in Support and requests.

The gift price is displayed in diamonds before sending. Confirmed gifts have a reference in wallet history. For an unexpected charge, open Purchase issue and include the reference. The store or payment provider shows the final checkout price.

Community rules apply to short videos and comments as well as rooms. Only publish content you have rights to share, including its music and images, and obtain the necessary permission from people who appear in it. By publishing a clip, you allow Clipi to store and display it and make it available for sharing and download within the service. Respect the creator’s rights when reusing a clip.

The gift panel displays the cost before you send. Video gifts spend purchased diamonds to support the clip. They do not generate earnings points or a withdrawable balance for the creator or their agency. Room gift rewards apply only to room gifts. Trial balances and simulated top-ups are for testing.

The monthly agency statement shows eligible room-gift diamonds and the host, agent and platform shares at the rates in effect when each gift was sent. Rate changes apply to future gifts. Dollar amounts are estimates of points. Actual withdrawal depends on the eligible balance and available payout methods.

Ludo, Jackaroo and pool in your profile are free to play. Play on a shared device or use your Clipi account to join an online table. Returning to the games menu preserves an active turn. Confirming departure from a running shared match ends the table for all players.

تظهر تكلفة الهدية بالماس قبل إرسالها. يعرض سجل المحفظة مرجع الهدية بعد تأكيدها. إذا وجدت خصمًا غير متوقع، استخدم «مشكلة في عملية شراء» وأرفق المرجع فقط. يعرض المتجر أو مزوّد الدفع السعر النهائي عند الشراء.

تنطبق قواعد المجتمع على المقاطع القصيرة والتعليقات كما تنطبق على الغرف. انشر فقط ما تملك حق نشره، بما فيه الموسيقى والصور، واحصل على الإذن اللازم ممن يظهر في المقطع. عند نشره، تسمح لكليبي بتخزينه وعرضه وإتاحة مشاركته وتنزيله ضمن الخدمة. احترم حقوق أصحاب المقاطع عند إعادة استخدامها.
